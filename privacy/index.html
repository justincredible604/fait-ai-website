FAiT Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

FAiT ("App" or "Service") is a personal AI fitness coach that helps you track workouts, nutrition, and health metrics. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect and use your data.

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by CPRA)
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CalOPPA (California Online Privacy Protection Act)

2. Personal Information We Collect

Account Registration Data:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • City, State/Province, ZIP/Postal code
  • Password (encrypted)

Health & Fitness Data:

  • Exercises logged (name, weight, reps, sets)
  • Meals logged (name, calories, macros)
  • Weight and body measurements
  • Steps, sleep hours, water intake
  • Fasting sessions
  • Workout photos
  • Voice recordings (transcribed to text, original audio not stored)

Social Media Profile Information:

When you connect via Facebook or Google:

  • Profile name
  • Profile picture
  • Email address
  • Basic profile information

Device & Usage Data:

  • Device type, OS version, app version
  • Pages/screens visited, features used
  • Interaction timing and frequency
  • Crash logs and error reports

Analytics Data (PostHog):

We use PostHog to track user behavior, feature usage, and app performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide personalized AI coaching
  • To generate fitness recommendations
  • To track your progress
  • To process in-app payments
  • To send transactional emails
  • To send promotional emails (with your consent)
  • To improve the app
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Email Communications

Transactional Emails: Account confirmations, password resets, purchase receipts.

Promotional Emails: You can opt-in during signup and opt-out anytime via the "Unsubscribe" link or Settings → Email Preferences.

5. In-App Payments & Advertising

Payment Methods:

  • Apple App Store In-App Payments
  • Google Play In-App Payments

Advertising: We may display ads in the future using non-personal data.

7. Data Storage & Security

  • Data stored on Supabase (US-based) with encryption at rest
  • All communication encrypted via HTTPS
  • Only you can access your account with your encrypted password
  • Industry-standard security practices

8. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while account is active
  • Health data: Retained for analysis and coaching
  • Analytics data: Retained for up to 12 months
  • Upon deletion: All data removed within 30 days

9. Your Privacy Rights

CCPA + CPRA (California Users)

You have the right to:

  • Know: What personal information we collect
  • Delete: Your personal information
  • Opt-out: Of sale or sharing of your data
  • Correct: Inaccurate information
  • Data portability: Receive your data in portable format
  • Non-discrimination: We won't discriminate for exercising these rights

To exercise rights: info@faitapp.com

GDPR (EU Users)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Your personal data
  • Rectification: Fix inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data portability: Export your data
  • Restrict processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Object: To processing
  • Lodge a complaint: With your data protection authority

To exercise rights: info@faitapp.com

CalOPPA (California Users)

  • Privacy Policy accessible in the app
  • We respond to privacy requests within 45 days
  • We do not track users across third-party websites
  • Security breaches reported without unreasonable delay

10. Children's Privacy

FAiT is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If we discover we have collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

Parents or guardians concerned their child has provided information should contact info@faitapp.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post updates in the app and notify users of material changes.
